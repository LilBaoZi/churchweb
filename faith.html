Our Statement of Faith

English

We believe in one God, who is the Creator of man and all things, absolutely perfect and eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

We believe that Jesus Christ is both true God and true man, having been conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary. He was crucified for our iniquities, was buried, on the third day arose bodily from the dead, and ascended into heaven. He is now our High Priest and Advocate at the right hand of God. He is the head of the church. He will come back again to marry His bride (Church) and install His kingdom.

We believe that the Holy Spirit is equal in substance and glory with God the Father and the Son. That the ministry of the Holy Spirit is to glorify the Lord Jesus Christ, and during this age to convict man, regenerate sinners, indwell, guide and empower believers for godly living and service, and build the body of Christ according to God’s will.

We believe the Scriptures, both the Old and New Testaments, are the inspired Word of God without error in the original writings, the complete revelation of His will for the salvation of man, the authority for Christian faith and life, and the guidance of church life, serving and worship.

We believe that man was created in the image of God, but fell into sin and is therefore lost, and can obtain salvation and spiritual life only through regeneration by the Holy Spirit.

We believe that salvation is by grace alone, through faith in the shed blood of Jesus Christ. Those who thus receive Jesus Christ by faith have their sins forgiven, are born of the Holy Spirit, become children of God, and are made a new creation in Christ.

We clearly understand that Jesus is above all and within all. Church does not have boundary of race, nationality, location and language. Every local church has the right under Christ to decide and govern its own affairs.
